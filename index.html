02. Research Interests

I'm interested in vision-based perception and learning for autonomous robotic systems in aerospace and other inaccessible environments. Here, cameras are often the most informative sensor available and also the least reliable. My goal is closed-loop autonomy for inspection, assembly, and manufacturing in space.

🛰️

Vision-based Perception for Space Robotics

Perception and learning for robots operating where direct human access is impossible. I'm especially interested in estimating the pose of non-cooperative spacecraft from monocular images, so a servicer can approach, inspect, or capture a target that has no markers and cannot communicate.

  • Pose Estimation
  • Computer Vision
  • Deep Learning
  • Space Robotics

Robot Localization & State Estimation

Working out where a robot is, and how far to trust that estimate, from noisy and imperfect sensors. I'm interested in localization and sensor fusion that combine cameras, IMUs, encoders, and other sensors, and that stay reliable when one of them degrades, whether that's glare in orbit or snow in a field trial.

  • Localization
  • State Estimation

  • Sensor Calibration

Building physics-based models and simulations of robots, vehicles, and processes, then calibrating them against real measurements so they can be trusted for prediction and control. I'm interested in pairing these models with perception so that autonomous systems can reason about, and act on, the physical world they observe.

04. Projects

Featured · In Progress

Tumbleweed: Non-cooperative Satellite Pose Estimation

Using Stanford's SPEED+ dataset to build and tune a computer vision and machine learning model that estimates the pose of a non-cooperative spacecraft (Tango) from monocular images. A keypoint heatmap maps the target's geometry and compares the relative locations of its features as seen by the vision pipeline. The validated pose estimates then feed a control model for satellite navigation and interception.

  • Computer Vision
  • Machine Learning
  • Pose Estimation
  • SPEED+
  • GNC
Autonomous Mobile Robot

SNAPPA: Vision-guided Navigation & Pick-and-place

Semi-autonomous Navigation and Actuation for Pneumatic Pick-and-place Applications. A mobile robot that uses an onboard camera as its primary feedback signal: an OpenCV pipeline detects the line and target, feeding PD heading control and PID wheel-speed control, and a Raspberry Pi state machine sequences navigation, alignment, and pneumatic actuation.

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • Visual Feedback Control
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Arduino

Encoder-based Caliper: Sensing & Calibration

A caliper-style measurement device that pairs a 3D-printed rack-and-pinion with a rotary encoder, calibrated to convert angular motion into accurate linear displacement.

  • Sensor Calibration
  • Embedded
  • CAD
